Subscriptions

AuraLunis Premium is a one-time lifetime purchase of $29.99. To check your purchase status or request a refund, go to Settings → Apple ID → Purchase History on your device, or visit Apple Support.

Common Questions

  • Sky Lens isn't aligned — Make sure Location Services are enabled for AuraLunis in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Motion & Fitness must also be enabled.
  • Stars look wrong for my location — AuraLunis computes sky positions from your GPS coordinates. Check that your location is accurate (outdoors works best).
  • Premium features not unlocking — Try restoring purchases from the AuraLunis Settings screen. If the issue persists, contact us with your Apple ID email.
  • App crashes or freezes — Make sure you're running the latest version from the App Store. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and iOS version.
